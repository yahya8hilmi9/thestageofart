About the club

A stage for everyone

The Stage of Arts Club is a community of young people who love theatre and music. In our workshops (Atelier de l'art) we play, improvise, sing, laugh and build performances together.

No experience needed. Just come with curiosity, energy and the will to express yourself.

What we do

Express. Perform. Create.

🎭

Theatre

Acting exercises, improvisation, body expression, voice work and scene creation, from first steps to full performances.

🎵

Music

Singing, rhythm and instruments. We create music that lives on stage and supports our plays.

✨

Creation

Writing, staging and creative projects. Every member helps shape the shows, on stage and behind the scenes.
